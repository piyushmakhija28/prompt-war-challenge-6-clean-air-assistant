# ADR-006: DIY Corsi-Rosenthal Box Filtration Physics & Community Economics

## Status
**ACCEPTED**

## Context
Commercial air purifiers cost between $200 and $400, making them completely inaccessible to low- and middle-income families across smog-afflicted urban areas. Unproven folklore remedies (e.g. herbal diffusers) offer zero particulate capture.

## Decision
We endorse and provide detailed engineering instructions for the **DIY Corsi-Rosenthal Box** (1x 20-inch box fan + 4x MERV 13 furnace filters + cardboard shroud).

## Consequences
### Positive
- **Proven Filtration Physics:** Delivers CADR of $\ge 270\text{ CFM}$, matching or outperforming commercial units.
- **Economic Inclusivity:** Costs $\approx \$30 - \$35$, allowing collective builds by schools and apartment communities.
- **Low Noise & High Airflow:** Multi-filter cube design reduces face velocity, reducing motor strain and acoustic noise.

### Negative & Mitigated
- **Filter Availability:** Mitigated by including alternatives for single-filter box fans and secondary damp microfiber protocols.
